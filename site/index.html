    05. Match Rules & FAQ
    
          
    Before you take guard.
    
          
    
            Key technical details about WebGPU requirements, game files, roster switching, and open-source credits.
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    
            Can I play on desktop and mobile?+
            
    Yes. Desktop Chrome or Edge with WebGPU supports keyboard and gamepad play in full widescreen. On mobile, touch controls with an on-screen stick and shot diamond are built in (iPhone requires iOS 26 WebGPU launched from Add to Home Screen).
    
          
    
          
    
            How do game files and rosters load?+
            
    When hosted game chunks are staged, the launcher downloads and verifies them automatically into browser storage (OPFS) and reuses them on later visits. In local mode, you can pick your own Cricket 07 folder containing Data, Audio, and the executable once.
    
          
    
          
    
            What happens to the original 2006 teams?+
            
    OG India and the classic national, county, and state squads remain protected. Secondary A-team and invitational slots power the expanded 2026 and IPL rosters, and you can switch back to Original teams anytime with separate save directories.
    
          
    
          
    
            Does it keep my saved games?+
            
    Yes. Native in-game saves persist inside your browser's private storage, and the launcher includes one-click JSON backup and restore controls before starting a match.
    
          
    
          
    
            Who built the underlying browser port?+
            
    The upstream static-recompilation browser port was built by Dhaiwat Pandya & contributors (including Stephan Cilliers’ scheduler, netplay, and controller work) under the MIT license.
